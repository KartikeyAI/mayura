# Support policy

Mayura `0.x` releases are development previews. They receive best-effort community support only, carry no response-time or maintenance SLA, and may contain incompatible changes documented in the changelog and migration notes. No `0.x` release is approved for production or hostile-code execution merely because its tests pass.

Only the environments in `docs/support-matrix.md` are qualified. Other environments may work but are unsupported until added with retained evidence. Security reports follow `SECURITY.md`; suspected vulnerabilities must not be posted in public issues.

The latest development preview is the only supported `0.x` line. Security fixes may require immediate upgrade and will not expand permissions or data destinations silently. Before 1.0, maintainers must publish a stable-version support window, deprecation period and end-of-support process.
